# Privacy

Markdown Minimal collects nothing, because there is nothing to collect.

- **No network requests.** The extension never talks to any server. The only URLs it touches are the ones it pastes into your Markdown.
- **No storage.** Nothing is saved — no preferences, no history, no analytics.
- **Clipboard only.** The Markdown it builds goes straight to your clipboard and nowhere else.
- **About links are plain links.** The about section at the bottom of the popup (my Twitter, Digital Meld, the SSTB.ai community) fetches nothing — clicking one just opens a normal browser tab, like any link on the web.
- **Minimal permissions.** `contextMenus`, `clipboardWrite`, `activeTab`, and host access to read the link, image, or text you right-clicked. That's the whole list.
